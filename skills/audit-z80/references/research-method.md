# Research method

Shared by audit, shrink, and optimize after their research trigger holds.
External material supplies hypotheses and verification methods, not local proof.

## Search and verify

1. Form a bounded question from exact local signatures: opcodes, symbols,
   generated code, tool/version/flags, model, address, symptom, asset, or budget.
2. Search fragments and concepts; vary vocabulary and relevant languages
   (English, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Czech, or community terms).
3. Diversify sources: toolchain source/tests/issues/commits; emulator tests and
   hardware measurements; repositories, codecs/reference decoders/benchmarks,
   forks, disassemblies, generators; regional forums, archives, blogs, mailing
   lists, postmortems, demoscene NFOs and adjacent Z80 communities.
4. Follow citations, authors, linked code, issues, forks, and archived links.
   Named sites are starting points, not an allowlist; snippets are not evidence.
5. Try to refute finalists: bugs, regressions, rejected issues, clone/model,
   interrupt, timing, and compatibility counterexamples.
6. Verify CPU/model, toolchain/ABI, memory, interrupts, paging, timing, and hidden
   costs before transfer. Prefer primary artifacts plus independent corroboration.

Search only minimal normalized signatures; never upload private source or
project identifiers. Popularity or forum agreement without local support is SEED.

## Research card

```text
Question / exact query:
URL / access date / revision / source class:
Claim or implementation / original target/version/preconditions:
Independent corroboration / current project anchor:
Local verification / domain-specific impact and costs:
Status: SEED | EXTERNALLY CORROBORATED | LOCALLY APPLICABLE | REJECTED
```

## Budget and stop

Keep at most three cards for Standard, five total for Deep; verify only the two
or three that can change the decision. Workflow alone controls delegation.
When permitted, give a research-scout bounded questions and a compact project
signature, not private source or other agents' conclusions; local lanes can
work independently on the immutable baseline. Rank cards by decision impact
and verification cost. Without a delegate, resolve the top question first.

Stop when sources converge and local verification is clear, results repeat
folklore, vocabulary expansion finds no local anchor, or further search cannot
change the decision, confidence, or residual risk. If browsing is unavailable,
retain exact pending queries and continue locally without inventing support.
Cite provenance beside the candidate; promotion requires current local evidence.
