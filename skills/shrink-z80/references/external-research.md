# External Research

Research when explicitly requested, a broad SAFE pass misses the byte/resident
goal, or codec, startup, ABI, toolchain, target, generated-code, renderer, loader,
audio, or bank behavior can change the best candidate. Skip when fresh local
evidence already proves the best path.

Read the [shared research method](../../audit-z80/references/research-method.md).
Include asset type/size/entropy, codec/decoder, workspace, load-time constraints,
CRT/clib, flags, helpers, and map sections in relevant queries.

Search lanes: startup/library deletion; representation and generators; codec
tradeoffs on actual assets; rendering; banks/overlays/IM2/ROM; specialized sound;
page-local tables, RST, SMC/SP/flags only after SAFE opportunities.

Add the net-byte formula, hidden decoder/workspace costs, and behavior/ABI/timing
checks to each card. Count the exact linked decoder/version; web sizes and cycle
counts are not local measurements. Reject candidates disproved by local counts;
stop when further research cannot change the ranking or requested reserve.
External leads remain `ESTIMADO` or `REQUIERE BUILD`; only current local
counts/builds establish `EXACTO`.
